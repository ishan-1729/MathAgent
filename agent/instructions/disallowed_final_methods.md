# Disallowed Final Methods

Final proof attempts must not rely on:

- UFDs in rings of algebraic integers;
- ideals or class groups;
- elliptic curves;
- modularity;
- algebraic geometry;
- p-adic theory beyond elementary congruences;
- Catalan/Mihailescu;
- Baker theory;
- computational brute force unless finite bounds are elementary and explicit.

Reading or summarizing such methods for inspiration is allowed; using them as final proof steps is not.
